about

I have spent years studying motivation, behavioral design, and engagement, then applying those ideas to real products. I co-founded CustomerGlu and built its gamification SDK from scratch; it eventually reached more than 150 million devices.

After CustomerGlu, I took one deliberate exploratory chapter: product and growth work at Lingotune, the One Person Growth Team, Distill, and a series of AI-assisted product experiments. The common thread was learning how a small, high-agency team could research, build, grow, and learn faster.

Today I prefer hands-on building to managing large teams. I work across product judgment, research, design, engineering, and the operating systems that let those disciplines compound.
