General Formation

General Formation takes a founder's intent and coordinates specialist agents to turn it into inspectable business work—not just an answer in a chat window.

role
Founding engineer
fields
agent systems · product · engineering

The question

Can a small company gain real operating leverage from agents without giving up founder judgment, taste, or visibility into what happened?

What I am building

I work across the product experience and the underlying constellation: planning, specialist handoffs, evaluation gates, resumable runs, and a terminal cockpit where a founder can steer the work.

A working receipt

One completed run turned the thin prompt “Build a business which creates green, healthy smoothies in Bali” into Akar: a grounded product wedge, brand system, social assets, and a rendered responsive site.

Truth boundary

Akar is evidence of the system producing a coherent business concept and launch-ready collateral. It is not an operating smoothie company, and I do not present it as one.
