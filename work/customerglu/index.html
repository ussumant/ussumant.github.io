CustomerGlu

CustomerGlu made it possible for consumer product teams to run native gamified journeys—streaks, challenges, rewards, and campaigns—without rebuilding the machinery each time.

role
Co-founder
fields
behavioral design · consumer products · company building

What I owned

I co-founded the company and built its gamification SDK and platform from scratch, working across product, engineering, behavioral design, customers, and the company itself.

The deeper thread

The work was an applied study of motivation: not gamification as decoration, but how feedback, goals, progress, rewards, and timing change what people do inside products.

Scale

The SDK eventually reached more than 150 million devices through consumer apps in India and Southeast Asia.
