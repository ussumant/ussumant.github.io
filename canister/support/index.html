canister · support

Canister is a film camera app. You shoot a roll without seeing your photos, drop it at the lab, and the prints come back the next morning at 8.

Common questions

  • When do my prints come back? At 8 am, at least six hours after you drop the roll off. The ticket on the desk says when.
  • I bought film and don't see it. Open the notebook on the desk and tap Restore purchases. Unlimited film comes back that way. Single rolls are used once, so if one is missing, write to me with the date and I'll sort it out.
  • A print didn't come out. The envelope tells you how many frames didn't. Write to me and say which roll.
