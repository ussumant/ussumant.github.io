canister · privacy

Canister is a film camera app. It has no account and no sign-in.

What stays on your phone

Every photo you take, your rolls, your prints and your scans. Canister keeps them in its own storage on your phone. They leave only when you save scans to Photos or share a print yourself.

What leaves your phone

When you buy film, Apple handles the payment, and Canister never sees your card. A record of the purchase (which product, when, and an ID made up for this app) goes to RevenueCat, which Canister uses to keep its sales records. That record carries no name or email.

What Canister doesn't do

No analytics, no ads, no tracking across other apps, no location.

Notifications

If you allow them, Canister tells you when your prints are back. They're scheduled on your phone; nothing is sent from a server.

Deleting

Delete the app and everything it kept on your phone goes with it. The purchase record at RevenueCat stays, still with no name or email. Write to me if you want it removed.
